Applications for the CCP4-BCA Protein Crystallography Summer School 2026 are currently open! It will run from the Sunday 6th-Friday 11th September and will be hosted at the John Innes Centre in Norwich. 

The deadline is the 19th June. If you are interested, please find more details [here](https://www.jic.ac.uk/event/ccp4-bca-protein-crystallography-summer-school-2026/).
