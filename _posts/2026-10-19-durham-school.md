---
title:  "Applications open for the BCA/CCG Intensive Teaching School in X-ray Structure Analysis"
date:   2026-10-19 09:35:00 +0100
categories: news
---

Applications are now open for the 21st  BCA/CCG Intensive Teaching School in X-ray Structure Analysis, which will run from the 3rd - 11th April 2027 in Durham, UK. The school is intended primarily for younger scientists with some experience of structure analysis who wish to improve their understanding of the underlying principles and practice.

More details about the school and registration can be found at [https://bcaccgschool.crystallography.org.uk/](https://bcaccgschool.crystallography.org.uk/). Registration will close on the 12th of January 2027.

If you have any queries or need further information, please email natalie.e.pridmore@durham.ac.uk.
